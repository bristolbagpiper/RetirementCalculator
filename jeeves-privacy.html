Jeeves privacy policy

This policy describes the private Jeeves Google integration. It does not describe the separate retirement calculator hosted on this domain.

Information accessed

Depending on the requested task or configured routine, the integration accesses Gmail message headers, sender and recipient details, subjects, bodies and attachments, and Google Calendar event information such as titles, dates, participants, locations and descriptions. OAuth credentials are stored in the operator’s local environment to allow authorised access and automatic token renewal.

Purpose and use

Google user data is used to provide the owner’s personal-assistant functions: correspondence review, schedule and meeting preparation, authorised email and calendar operations, personal document archiving, and commitment tracking. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used by this integration to develop or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

Processing and service providers

Relevant message or event content may be sent to the hosted AI provider selected in Hermes, currently including OpenAI, for inference needed to perform the owner’s tasks. That processing is subject to the applicable provider’s service and data-handling terms. Google provides Gmail, Calendar and OAuth services. Configured personal alerts may be delivered through Telegram, and authorised reports may be delivered by email. These transfers are for the requested assistant functions; public visitors to this website do not receive Google user data.

Storage, retention and deletion

Selected documents, source references, commitments, assistant history and operational records may be stored locally and retained until the operator removes them. Retention is not governed by a universal automatic deletion period. Revoking Google access stops future authorised API access but does not automatically delete previously retained records, backups, delivered email or messages. The operator can delete local retained information and review deletion options for records held by service providers.

Security and control

The integration uses OAuth rather than storing a Google account password. Tokens and private records are not published on this website. The owner controls the assistant environment and can revoke the integration in Google Account’s third-party access settings at any time. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Google API Limited Use

Jeeves’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is accessed and transferred only for the personal-assistant functions described here, and not for unrelated purposes.

Questions and changes

This integration is operated privately by its account owner, who manages access, retained information and deletion directly. This policy will be updated when the integration’s data practices materially change.
