Jeeves — personal Google integration

Jeeves is a private personal assistant, running on Hermes Agent by Nous Research. This integration is for the operator’s own Google account. It is not a public service and visitors cannot connect their accounts here.

What the integration does

With the account owner’s authorisation, Jeeves accesses Gmail and Google Calendar to prepare email and diary briefings, identify correspondence requiring attention, retain useful personal documents and track unresolved commitments. Configured routines can send briefings to the owner and deliver personal reminders.

Google permissions

The existing OAuth client requests Gmail read, modify and send permissions, and Google Calendar access. These permissions support reading mail and events, sending authorised briefings or replies, and making account-owner-directed changes. Granting access does not itself instruct the assistant to send, delete or modify anything.

How it operates

The integration runs in the operator’s personal assistant environment, not in this website. Relevant content may be processed by hosted AI services used by Hermes, currently including OpenAI, to carry out the owner’s requested tasks. Useful records and credentials are retained in the operator’s local environment.

Read the privacy policy for data handling and the terms of use for the personal-use scope.
